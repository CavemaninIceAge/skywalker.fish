When I wake and draw the curtains again, a new crescent hangs like a hook at the edge of the sky.

Perhaps life finds its coherence between strength and tenderness. It is not necessarily good to be alert, shrewd, and quick-witted all the time. Listening now to the traffic and wind outside, I feel something like the redemption of home. Sometimes we move slowly through time and are quietly pleased to do so.

But over these next few days, I must concentrate and avoid unnecessary distractions. Also, happiness in life must come from reality, not from indulgent substances.

“Six days until the gaokao.”
