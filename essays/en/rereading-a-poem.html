When cobwebs ruthlessly seal off my hearth,

When smoke from the ashes sighs with poverty’s sorrow,

I still stubbornly smooth the ashes of disappointment,

And write with beautiful snowflakes: believe in the future.


When my purple grapes turn to late-autumn dew,

When my flowers nestle in someone else’s heart,

I still stubbornly take a withered, frost-bound vine,

And write on the desolate earth: believe in the future.


I will make fingers of the waves surging to the horizon,

I will make a palm of the sea that lifts the sun,

And sway the warm, lovely pen of dawn,

To write in a child’s hand: believe in the future.


The reason I believe so firmly in the future

Is that I believe in the eyes of those yet to come—

Their lashes can brush away the dust of history,

Their pupils can see through the pages of the years.


Whether, for our rotting flesh,

Our sorrow in losing the way, our pain in failure,

People offer moved tears and profound sympathy,

Or contemptuous smiles and biting ridicule,


I firmly believe that, for our backbone,

Our countless searches, wrong turns, failures, and successes,

They will offer a warm, objective, and fair judgment.

Yes, I wait impatiently for their judgment.


Friends, believe firmly in the future.

Believe in unyielding effort.

Believe in youth that triumphs over death.

Believe in the future. Love life.
