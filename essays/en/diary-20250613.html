I feel increasingly how important intelligence is. I have a fairly abundant supply of it. If I overcome my bad habits and reestablish my own agency after years of emotional isolation, I will stand alongside the wisest people in the world. I should keep working toward that goal.

Believe in the power of accumulation. Because its results cannot appear instantly, I should build habits of learning and progress.

And to overcome this state of emotional isolation, I must push through the headaches and write The Road Not Taken well.

Set a schedule:

Get up early and go swimming. Read, write, and study English in the morning.

Sleep at noon. Go to Jile Temple and work out in the afternoon.

After eight in the evening, talk to people and go out.

Go to bed at eleven. Arrange separate times for tutoring and learning an instrument.
