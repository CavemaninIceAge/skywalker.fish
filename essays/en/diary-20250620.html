So much for fucking going to bed early at eight. I woke up at midnight. I spent two hours writing The Road Not Taken, then saw a message from S. I think I really have never been a particularly important member of his inner circle. Perhaps I am. I simply never joined that circle.

I do envy him for having a circle of his own. But I have also succeeded in building my own life. Even though I honestly envy his more.

S is a mirror through which I see the world. Again and again, he reminds me how the people who seem to be around me actually think and live. His presence has become an important reference in my own process of modernization, on the way to becoming better.

My envy of him and my acknowledgment of his positive influence may resonate deeply when I look back over my life. I truly am someone precocious yet late to mature. The road has been twisting and rough. I love my modern life, and I also love the gifts my unusual path of growth has given me.

Things are good now. I am moving forward and feel more at home in my own life. Perhaps this is the ultimate meaning of “humble progressivism”: to love life and see what lies ahead; neither struggle from a sense of inferiority before outside standards nor cling to the life you already have. Neither servile nor arrogant, cultivate your own tomorrow.
