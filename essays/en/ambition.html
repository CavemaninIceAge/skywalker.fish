I am so fond of the ambitious streak in me. You could also call it a pursuit, a desire for control or a sense of command…

Like the Phantom descending with undeniable force through the surging music as the great bell collapses in The Phantom of the Opera; like the young Hamilton walking the streets of New York, wanting to master every adverse circumstance and burning to show what he could do; like Julien hanging Napoleon’s portrait high in his bedroom, wanting glory to bow before him, yearning to rise above his station until every small favor seemed insignificant.

The longing to distinguish myself wraps so tightly around me. Yesterday Mum was telling me about the bright prospects of a career in psychology, the middle-class life of earning nearly a thousand yuan an hour. I said:

“No matter how much you earn, you’re still serving other people. Small favors mean nothing to me…”

At that moment Julien’s shadow almost perfectly overlapped my own: inferiority, pride, ambition, sensitivity, intoxication with the magnificent prospect of every honor arriving at once. Meanwhile, my sense of worth, held down in the present, makes my whole spirit more excited and restless…
