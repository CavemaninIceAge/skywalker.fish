But by today, everything has recovered. I've handled it all well, and there isn't much I'm dissatisfied with. I feel that among the starting conditions of my life, very few were excellent from the outset, and not many were even good. More were merely passing, or nowhere near passing. These things once troubled me. But as I've grown, I've learned to face them, change them, and ‘get things right.’

Avoidance solves nothing. And with some problems, conceding defeat only means suffering even more painful consequences afterward. In certain overwhelming predicaments, I defend some core interests and tell myself: “We shall fight on the beaches. We shall fight on the landing ground. We shall fight in the fields and in the streets. We shall fight in the hills. We shall never surrender.”

At other times, when I have more room to breathe, I tell myself: “Not because they are easy, but because they are hard. Because that goal will serve to organize and measure the best of our energies and skills. Because that challenge is one that we are willing to accept. One we are unwilling to postpone. And one which we intend to win.”
