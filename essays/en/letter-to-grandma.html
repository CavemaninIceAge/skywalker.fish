Grandma, I caught a cold on Tuesday. Let me use this time to tell you a little about life at home.

Take today, for example. I got up at the usual time, half past six. It was the fourth day of my cold and I felt awful, so I said I would rest and go to school in the afternoon. I could not sleep anyway, so I got up at seven, organized some recent things, and waited to eat. But at 7:40 neither of them was up. I went to wake Mum, and she said the food was on the table. I looked. It was the usual school-day breakfast she prepares for me to eat in the car, and it was cold.

In October Mum always made one bacon sandwich for breakfast. At the end of October I told her one was not enough to fill me up, so during the first half of November she packed two at my request. I even praised her. But around November 20 it inexplicably went back to one a day. At the beginning of December I said one was not enough, and asked why she did not keep packing two. She said she understood, but around December 10 she began giving me only one sandwich again. The day before yesterday I told her one really did not fill me up and asked if she could pack two. When I checked my breakfast today, there was still only one.

I really started to break down. I began wondering whether she could understand plain language, whether there was something wrong with her comprehension, or whether she was simply that lazy.

So I ate the sandwich. I was sleepy and sick and wanted to sleep after they went out. Dad had to go to the Qunli campus of Harbin No. 3 High School after nine this morning to observe and evaluate a lesson. I lay in my room listening to the chaos of them getting ready, walking back and forth all over the apartment, again and again. They did not leave until 8:40.

That was the second thing today that really upset me. I honestly do not understand how people can be so disorganized that leaving the house takes forty minutes.

After nine I slept a little and took a shower. They came home at noon; by then I had finished showering and sat down at my desk. I recently got a new desk, dark-brown solid wood, so white cat hair shows up especially clearly. There was a lot of cat hair on it. Mum was cooking noodles. I asked, “Didn’t I tell you not to let the cat into my room?” Why was there cat hair? She kept cooking without answering. I asked if she had heard me. She said she had, and that she had not let the cat into my room. I said, Then why is there cat hair on the desk? She said she did not know.

This was not the first time. Before the desk even arrived, I told them it had seams in the solid wood and that cat hair getting into them would be impossible to deal with. But when I came home the day it arrived, it was still covered in cat hair. I asked, Didn’t I tell you not to let the cat in? She said she had let it in.

I was so angry, the kind of anger that leaves you gasping and wanting to die on the spot. Are they fucking idiots? Can they not understand words, or what?

At noon the cat vomited on the living-room table. Dad was sprawled lazily on the sofa playing games and did not even look. The cat left a puddle of vomit on the table, and nobody has cleaned it up even now.

I have not argued with them today, or even in the past month. I have not so much as shouted. But I am honestly becoming hysterical. How can they be so incapable of understanding, so lazy, slow, vacant, and stupid? I really cannot take it anymore. I think I am a pretty good communicator; everyone can tell. But now I am so angry I can hardly breathe, so hysterical, so close to breaking down. I cannot take it anymore.

There are only 169 days until the college entrance exam. I can honestly say I am giving it everything I have. They affect my state of mind so badly and put so much pressure on me. I am falling apart.

There may not be much you can do. I just wanted to tell you. I know you understand me, and that you really approach life positively. I hope you can help me talk through this…

To say it plainly, living with them makes me feel clogged up, miserable, nauseated, ready to throw up…
