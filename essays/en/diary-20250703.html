I got up at eight this morning and discussed university applications with the Tsinghua admissions team, the Peking University admissions team, my eldest aunt, my sister, and several of my aunt’s knowledgeable friends and relatives. A few of my sister’s main points struck me: ‘University is about experiencing life’; ‘If you want to do postgraduate study abroad, people overseas don’t understand all the tiny distinctions we make’; ‘Peking University’s policies on majors seem more accommodating.’ So I decided on the Economics Department at Peking University.

People should live for what they believe in or love. Otherwise, existence has no value.
