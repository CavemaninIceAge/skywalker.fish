I cycled from Siping to Kaiyuan. By the time I finished dinner, the sky had turned hazy and dim. I put aside my plan to continue to Tieling and found an inexpensive business hotel for the night. When I went in, there was a red cushion with OYOYOYOYO… repeated across it. It made my heart lurch.

On May 21, I went to school without cigarettes, and by four o’clock I couldn’t stand it anymore. I’d forgotten my key too, so I took my ID and some money to a small hotel near the school to rest for a while. The owner played a strange game with words. Numbly, I handed over another forty yuan, went out to the dusty balcony to smoke, then lay down for what felt like a long season. That hotel was like any ordinary small roadside hotel. That bed also had a red cushion printed with OYOYOYOYO. After that, I never stayed in places like it again. And in the red of that cushion, I discovered for the first time that blood made me faint.

“The overpass was torn down, a new tunnel built; I walk and walk, but never leave my Yancang Bridge.”
