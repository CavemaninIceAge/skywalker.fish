I so envy the romances you had in your school days.

You didn’t yet have to worry so much about tomorrow or what other people thought. You hadn’t mastered the techniques or the lines, and had no idea what came after passion. You were simply bathed in those hazy feelings, free to quietly wonder what someone else was thinking.

“I never saw the school beside the rice paddies. I never saw you at fourteen or fifteen.”

And young bodies are healthy, with hormones flowing. Every flutter, every nervous moment, every joy and sadness arrives with such intensity. There is still so much time to come together, break apart, and pull at each other.

But in my school days, I was just a shy, insecure little boy who didn’t know how to talk or even take care of his appearance. All I could do was watch you and imagine the experiences I might have had…
