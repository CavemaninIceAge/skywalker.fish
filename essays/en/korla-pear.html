I have had something like the flu for two days. My throat is rough and sore. I slept on and off for half the day and developed a fever at noon.

I try to feel what is different about being ill this time. Today I simply lay in bed, thinking of nothing. Just now I got up again and went to the washroom to wash the pear I brought from the cafeteria at lunch. I put on music I like and quietly ate my green Korla pear, one bite at a time. Life seemed rather pleasant.

Perhaps strength and indifference are not what growing up really means. Perhaps it is gentleness and ease.
