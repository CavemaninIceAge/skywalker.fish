It is already the early hours of the next day. I should record today’s lessons for future reference:

1. People differ in intelligence. Often you can tell at a glance. S can see it in S, just as I can tell on first meeting D and Y that they are not very bright. By the same measure, H, S, and Z look immediately perceptive, and in fact they are. I may not possess the sort of talent that astonishes the world. But through careful daily practice, recovering my vitality, and resisting the things that drain it, I will certainly be able, at my best, to perform as they ordinarily do. I have never broken through that barrier, but this fortress has gradually moved from the horizon to right before my eyes. I have cleared away many obstacles ahead of me. Within six months, I will reach a point where my best state matches the ordinary state of the most intelligent people in the world. This is no prophecy of doom. It is a goal already being realized.

2. Vitality is a very important quality. Sustained vitality can become a habit and an important source of energy for self-improvement. I did not rest well last night. This morning I went to Jile Temple, then spent an entire intense day with S. I am not tired now. Instead I feel the peaceful pleasure that follows a peak. I often encountered this situation in high school, but I treated my family’s low-energy state as normal and frequent rest as necessary. As a result, I often seemed to be left behind by the world. In fact, when we actively engage with life at every moment, we are least tired. This state should be sustained over time and established in our everyday awareness. It is an extremely important quality that I have always lacked. (Sustained vitality is extremely important!)

3. Ability always determines a person’s standing. Today I met X. At our small table of five, I suddenly realized that I was someone as important as S, someone whose words carried weight. Perhaps that comes from having so much to back me up. I could also see that S was happy to introduce me to more of his friends. I think I will need stronger achievements to gain an advantage in the long struggle. We should calmly accept the setbacks and lack of recognition encountered in pursuing power and status. At those moments, that is the treatment we can obtain. Yet those are also the circumstances in which we grow fastest and introduce ourselves to the most new people. We can learn more from people who are better than us, even when they are arrogant.

4. Stay sincere. Only by being true to our own thoughts can we express ourselves with vitality and live with vitality.

5. I need to build a social circle centered on myself too. I have seen Z’s circle and S’s circle. Each is the soul of his own social circle. One day I too will build a circle centered on me. That requires confident, sincere expression!

6. All of the above came from spending time with S. This shows that I must continue to spend time widely with people better than me. There is always so much new to learn.
