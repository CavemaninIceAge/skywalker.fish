Today is my eleventh day at Beijing Normal University. I have completely forgotten the miserable life at Deqiang. The two lives seem equally difficult, except that at Deqiang I had no freedom, and at Beijing Normal University I have no future.

The real pain here is that the teachers and students are so much weaker than those in my former surroundings. It feels as though I have stepped into a world like that of a certain secondary school in Harbin.
