My journey, Yu Tianxing’s, closely resembles that of the People’s Republic. At their core, both are about growing strong in isolation, then learning and accommodating the world’s rules from a foundation of self-reliance. Understanding the Republic’s journey is also a way to understand the eight years of my life since I became self-aware. The struggle of repeating my final school year is, in its nature, like the War of Resistance Against Japan: both a war of national liberation and a national democratic revolution.
