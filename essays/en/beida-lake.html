I came to Beijing depressed and restless, wanting to see the sea in Tanggu, but found a lake at Peking University instead.

We cycled around the lake. I said that place looked like the place from “After the laughter, what then?”

We sat on the island in the lake. I like S’s sense of inner order. Sitting beside the water, S said that what we live for in life are those moments that can be described in a handful of sentences.

S said he liked the month before the college entrance exam, when he could devote himself completely to a single goal.

Peking University is not as good as I imagined, but its stature is tangible.

“It is not reality that sustains your dreams, but your dreams that sustain your reality.”
