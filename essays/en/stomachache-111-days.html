I should not have eaten that fucking burger last night. I felt nauseated as soon as I got up after five today, came home from school after nine, and started vomiting on and off.

Before morning class had even begun, Little Sun said he wanted to find a history teacher to tutor me. The most absurd reversal yet.

Yesterday we passed his notebook around. After some grand declarations, he had specifically circled my name and Li Fangshi’s.

Back home I collapsed onto the bed and scrolled through videos until I saw this: a football match at the disability games. The ball flew straight over a player. He threw away his crutches and launched a beautiful bicycle kick into the air. The ball went into the goal, and he tumbled to the ground.

“The moment he let go of the crutches, whether it went in no longer mattered.”

I suddenly sat up, wanting to say all this. I do not know what I am getting at.
