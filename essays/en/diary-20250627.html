1. I must maintain the habit of writing a summary when the day’s work is done, linking the progress diary with going to sleep and resting. This will help establish a better evening routine.

Over the past few days, I have followed the new rules of action I drew up two days earlier: “sustained vitality, confidence and openness, embrace randomness.” They have worked very well for obtaining information, connecting with others, enriching life, and increasing happiness. I should continue to follow them.

3. I think that once I become aware of something, I can achieve it. Take sustained vitality. Yesterday morning I practiced guitar for two hours, attended a guitar lesson, discussed Peking University admissions, reconciled with G, played badminton with W, and had dinner and sang with W. That was the day. Today I got up and quickly memorized various chords, went to class on time, did extra practice, took a short nap at home, studied singing, worked out, and visited people I knew on Ashihe Street. I finally got home around half past seven. Such a life is full and happy, and not at all exhausting. It even helps greatly in avoiding indulgent, excessive rest.

4. “Anyone who goes out into the world to do things will have both wins and lows.” Although I did not get an ideal gaokao score, I think it is enough. And L, H, L, and others in this world have not achieved their dreams either. Imperfection is the normal state of the world. What matters is that we keep improving ourselves, so we have no regrets and raise the overall range within which our fortunes rise and fall. For everyone, at every time, imperfection is normal. Viewed across a lifetime, there will only be one or two truly, wonderfully good things. W was top of our year in middle school, yet today he is clearly not doing as well as I am. Today we stand on equal ground. “Anyone who goes out into the world to do things will have both wins and lows.” But as long as we keep improving, we will win in the long struggle. We should not confine our vision to a single moment or event.
