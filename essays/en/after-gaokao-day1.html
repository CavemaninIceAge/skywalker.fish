“You say you’ve been treated unfairly, but everyone’s life is more or less the same.”

The first day after the college entrance exam: doubt Wang Yinghan, understand Wang Yinghan, become Wang Yinghan. I had always wanted to go wild and have fun, but now that the holiday is here I have nothing to do. Somehow I already want to go to work or class. A welcome change.

For the coming month I have only a few projects: fitness training, singing lessons, guitar lessons, writing The Road Not Taken, and delivering food to keep my body and mind in balance. There are not many, but doing any of them well requires a great deal of time. I know that after today, everything should get back on track.

First I need to rent a little electric motorbike. It would be useful for getting around, earning money, passing the time, and exercise. At least I would not spend every day at home gaming and scrolling through videos. But writing this brings up an old incident. Before renting a bike last year, I wanted to buy one. Those two women sold me a bike rated for 15–25 kilometers per hour as though it were 15–25 miles per hour. When I wanted to return it, they acted as if they could not understand a word and argued with me. Eventually I did manage to return it, but I was called a fucking idiot. I could not persuade them. Even when the police came, all they could do was get everyone to leave peacefully.

I think that without the backing of status, you get sneered at and bullied in this world. With influence, life goes much more smoothly. But to me, that does not make the former life unworthy of living. It develops important abilities for success too: the spirit to stay ambitious when poor, the skill to handle complex situations, and the courage to keep your self-respect while being rejected.

Fine. No food delivery. I’ll tutor. I do not want to keep bowing and scraping while people look down on me. Both hardship and comfort have their reasons in this world. What matters is standing by my own beliefs. Besides, tutoring pays a lot. Maybe I’ll even date a high-schooler, hahaha.

Perhaps my greatest gain in recent years has been becoming my own best friend. Nothing off-limits, always supportive, always on the same side.

What is especially encouraging is that I am getting better at protecting my own interests, standing more firmly on my own side, maneuvering through situations with something of Liu Bang’s commanding ambition.

Conflict and struggle with my family have run through the last few years. I have learned not to be disrupted by other people’s emotions, while keeping my capacity for empathy. I am good at analyzing and solving problems, empathizing with myself, and protecting myself as well as possible in every relationship.

Ah, every time I drink, I mess things up. No more drinking~
