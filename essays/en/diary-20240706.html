Light rain in Tai’an County. I cannot set out. I watched a video series on conversation skills and tried them twice in real exchanges, with remarkably good results. Talking with my aunt last night, I also felt an objectivity and rationality I have never experienced at home. Life really ought to be like this. I rather regret not applying to Renmin University of China at the start. I should have come to Beijing earlier and, with my aunt’s help, raised myself to another level, away from the unstructured, purely emotional venting that passes for communication with my father and grandmother at home.

I should not repeat my final school year. I should get myself out of this low-level, draining struggle with my family as soon as possible. Interacting with them this way will only destroy me.
