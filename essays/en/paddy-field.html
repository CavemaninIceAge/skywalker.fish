One night long ago, when I foolishly went wandering around somebody else’s hometown, this was the little tune I was listening to. The route was twenty-six kilometers, the first time I had ever ridden more than ten. The Songhua River was ten kilometers wide along that stretch. The occasional searchlight beside the paddy fields illuminated the clouds directly overhead. They looked like white mica inside amethyst, enormous and astonishing.

Ever since, hearing this little tune has stirred a wish to pass through reality. To step into that world I can only touch by stretching my hand high, to enter a world that was once unknown and, without effort, would remain unknown forever, instead of staying here, staying here…
